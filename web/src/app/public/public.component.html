01 / The idea

Find your flight.
Leave the tab.

You already know where you want to go. There shouldn’t be a second job in watching the price.

Which flight links work?
  1. 01

    Paste the link.

    Choose a supported itinerary or route search on Google Flights. Add its link to your flight list.

  2. 02

    Set your signal.

    Watch for a new observed low, or choose a price you’d be happy to book. Connect Telegram for alerts.

  3. 03

    Get on with your day.

    We check periodically and keep the history. When an alert arrives, follow the flight link to check availability.

02 / Your kind of good price

A useful signal.
Then, back to life.

Choose what matters to you. Your flight list keeps the last observation, its time, and the lowest price seen while you’ve been watching.

Checks currently start at 15-minute intervals. Fares can change between checks; collection delays are shown in your dashboard.

03 / Made for two. Open to more.

It started with
a flight for Dad.

A price-drop email at midnight wasn’t much help if the fare had already bounced back. So we built a small tracker for a father and son, with alerts in a shared Telegram chat.

Fareflare grew from that. The same simple idea, now with your own flight list, your own alerts, and code you can read.
