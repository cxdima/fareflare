Before you add a flight

A few things to know about tracking with Fareflare.

Do I need to connect my Google account?

No. Paste a supported Google Flights link. Fareflare checks the public price without accessing your Google account.

Will I catch every price change?

Checks start at 15-minute intervals, so a fare can change between observations. Your dashboard shows the last recorded price and when it was checked. Always confirm the price before booking.

How do I receive an alert?

Choose a new observed low or a target price. Alerts appear in your dashboard. Connect Telegram in Account settings for messages, or configure a signed webhook for your own application.

Can I book tickets through Fareflare?

Fareflare tracks prices. When you find a fare you like, open the flight link to review availability and book with your chosen provider.
