# Code of Conduct

Fareflare follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies to the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and to
anyone representing the project elsewhere.

## In short

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Discuss the code, not the person.
- Welcome newcomers and questions. Nobody is expected to know everything.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination, sexualized language or imagery,
  doxxing, or sustained disruption.
- Keep examples free of other people's private information, including travel
  details, account names and chat identifiers.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub's private report form](https://github.com/cxdima/fareflare/security/advisories/new),
marking it as a conduct report, or by contacting the maintainer directly through
their GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews every report and may warn, remove content, or temporarily
or permanently restrict participation, in proportion to the impact and following
the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant. A participant named in a
report does not take part in handling it.
